1. Executive Summary & Double-Entry Architecture

The Finance & Accounts module of B2P ONE Portal & ERP is a complete, real-time, double-entry financial suite designed for media advertising, mobile roadshows, LED fleet campaigns, and agency operations.

The Golden Rule of Double-Entry Accounting:
For every single financial transaction posted in the ERP, the total debits must exactly equal the total credits:
Total Debits (Dr.) ≡ Total Credits (Cr.)
The system prevents posting unbalanced vouchers to safeguard company books against mathematical discrepancies.

3. Accounts Dashboard

The operational cockpit providing high-level financial metrics and immediate action shortcuts.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Displayed

  • Total Invoiced: Cumulative value of all approved tax invoices generated across campaigns.
  • Total Collected: Realized cash and bank collections received from customers.
  • Accounts Receivable (AR): Total unpaid balance owed by clients with overdue badges.
  • Accounts Payable (AP): Total unpaid vendor bills with 15/30-day payment schedules.
  • Total Expenses: Cumulative fleet, fuel, generator, and operating expenditure.
  • Net Profit: Real-time net operating margin (Revenue - Fleet COGS - Overhead Expenses).
  • Net GST Payable: Output GST collected from clients minus Input Tax Credit eligible.

Dashboard Action Buttons & Shortcuts

  • + Record Payment: Quickly log customer receipts without navigating to individual client profiles.
  • + Record Expense: Log instant cash or card expenses (fuel slips, toll, maintenance).
  • + Record Purchase: Enter a new supplier bill.
  • + Add Supplier: Register a new equipment or service vendor.
  • View Statement: Instantly opens the customer's or vendor's chronological ledger.

4. Chart of Accounts (COA) Structure & Master Setup

Every accounting transaction must be categorized into an account head. B2P ERP uses a standard 4-digit numeric system structured into five classes:

1000 - ASSETS (Normal Balance: Debit) ├── 1100 Current Assets │ ├── 1110 Cash in Hand / Petty Cash Drawer │ ├── 1120 Bank Accounts (HDFC Current Account, SBI, Federal Bank) │ └── 1130 Accounts Receivable (Sundry Debtors) └── 1200 Fixed Assets ├── 1210 LED Digital Vehicles (Vans, 3-Side Trucks) ├── 1220 LED Video Wall Panels & Processors └── 1230 Office Furniture, Computers & Production Equipment 2000 - LIABILITIES (Normal Balance: Credit) ├── 2100 Current Liabilities │ ├── 2110 Accounts Payable (Sundry Creditors) │ ├── 2120 Output CGST Payable │ ├── 2121 Output SGST Payable │ ├── 2122 Output IGST Payable │ └── 2130 Customer Advances / Deposits └── 2200 Long-Term Loans & Vehicle Financing 3000 - EQUITY & CAPITAL (Normal Balance: Credit) ├── 3100 Partner / Owner Capital Accounts └── 3200 Retained Earnings & Accumulated Surplus 4000 - REVENUE & INCOME (Normal Balance: Credit) ├── 4100 LED Van Advertising Revenue ├── 4200 LED Video Wall Rental Revenue ├── 4300 Mobile Roadshow & Campaign Services └── 4400 Signage, Flex Printing & Fabrications 5000 - EXPENSES (Normal Balance: Debit) ├── 5100 Direct Fleet & Campaign Costs (COGS) │ ├── 5110 Vehicle Fuel & Diesel │ ├── 5120 Driver & Technician Campaign Wages │ ├── 5130 Generator Fuel & Power Charges │ └── 5140 Sound, Lighting & Staging Hire └── 5200 Operating & Administrative Expenses ├── 5210 Office Rent, Electricity & Internet ├── 5220 Staff Salaries & Allowances ├── 5230 Vehicle Insurance, Fitness & RTO Taxes └── 5240 Repairs, Spares & Screen Servicing

Step-by-Step: Adding a New Account Head

  1. Go to Chart of Accounts.
  2. Click + New Account Head.
  3. Enter Account Code (e.g. 5150), Account Name (e.g. Campaign Sound System Hire), and select the parent Account Group (e.g. Expenses).
  4. Enter Opening Balance (if onboarding starting balances as of April 1st).
  5. Click Save Account.

5. General Journal, Vouchers & Rules

The General Journal is used for transactions that do not originate from standard billing, such as asset depreciation, salary accruals, contra transfers, and month-end adjustments.

Voucher Types Supported

Voucher Type Prefix When to Use
Journal Voucher JV Non-cash entries, depreciation, bad debt provisions, correction entries.
Contra Voucher CV Internal cash-to-bank deposits or bank-to-cash petty cash withdrawals.
Payment Voucher PV Direct payments made via Bank/Cash without a supplier bill.
Receipt Voucher RV Direct miscellaneous receipts (interest income, capital injection).

Step-by-Step: Posting a Journal Entry

  1. Click + New Journal Entry in the General Journal module.
  2. Select Voucher Type, Voucher Date, and enter a Reference Number.
  3. Add line items: Select Account Head, enter Debit or Credit amount, and type a line narration.
  4. Ensure that Total Debits equal Total Credits (Difference must show ₹0.00).
  5. Enter overall Voucher Narration and click Post Voucher.

6. Banking & Cash Management

Provides complete control over cash registers, petty cash drawers, bank books, and reconciliation.

1. Cash Book vs. Bank Book

  • Cash Book: Tracks physical currency movements for daily fleet expenditures, driver batta, and cash collections.
  • Bank Book: Tracks electronic transactions (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI, Cheques) across company bank accounts.

2. Contra Entries (Internal Fund Transfers)

When drawing cash from the bank to replenish the petty cash drawer:

Debit: 1110 Petty Cash Drawer ₹ 25,000 (Cash Inflow) Credit: 1120 HDFC Current Account ₹ 25,000 (Bank Outflow) Narration: Cash withdrawal for Palakkad LED van operations (Cheque #104421)

3. Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS)

Staff must perform monthly bank reconciliation to ensure the ERP bank balance matches the actual bank statement:

  1. Open Banking & Cash $ ightarrow$ Select Bank Account $ ightarrow$ Click Start Reconciliation.
  2. Enter Statement Ending Date and Statement Ending Balance from netbanking statement.
  3. Mark transactions as Cleared as they appear on your physical bank statement.
  4. The ERP calculates:
    Statement Balance + Deposits in Transit - Outstanding Cheques = ERP Ledger Balance
  5. When the Difference is ₹0.00, click Finalize Reconciliation.

8. Expenses Management (Fleet & Operations)

Designed for rapid entry of day-to-day fleet and operating expenses without creating formal vendor bills.

  1. Go to Expenses $ ightarrow$ Click + Record Expense.
  2. Category: Select Vehicle Fuel & Diesel, Vehicle Repairs & Spares, Driver Batta, etc.
  3. Paid Through: Choose Petty Cash or HDFC Debit Card.

9. Suppliers Directory (Vendor Master)

The central master database for all subcontractors, fabricators, screen manufacturers, and fleet workshops.

Key Supplier Fields

  • Supplier Name: Official registered trade name.
  • GSTIN & PAN: Required for GST Input Tax Credit and TDS compliance.
  • Credit Terms: Standard credit window in days (e.g. 15 Days, 30 Days, or Immediate).
  • Bank Details: Vendor Bank Name, Account Number, and IFSC Code for NEFT payouts.
  • Supplier Ledger: Click View Ledger to see total historical purchases, payments made, and current payable balance.

10. Purchases & Payables (Accounts Payable - AP)

Tracks all formal vendor bills, fabricator invoices, equipment purchases, and payment settlements.

1. Recording a Vendor Purchase Bill

  1. Go to Purchases & Payables $ ightarrow$ Click + New Purchase Bill.
  2. Select Supplier from dropdown.
  3. Enter Vendor Bill Number and Bill Date.
  4. Set Payment Due Date (auto-calculated from credit days).
  5. Add Line Items: Select Expense/Asset Account (e.g. 5140 Sound & Light Equipment Hire), HSN/SAC code, quantity, unit rate, and GST rate (5%, 12%, 18%).
  6. Click Save & Post Bill.
Accounting Entry Behind the Scenes: Debit: 5140 Sound & Equipment Hire ₹ 50,000 (Direct Expense) Debit: 1140 Input GST Tax Credit ₹ 9,000 (ITC Receivable - 18%) Credit: 2110 Accounts Payable ₹ 59,000 (Liability to Vendor)

2. Paying a Vendor Bill

  1. On the purchase bill row, click Pay Vendor.
  2. Select source account (Bank Account or Cash in Hand) and Payment Mode (NEFT/Cheque/Cash).
  3. Enter UTR / Cheque reference and payment amount.
  4. Click Confirm Disbursement.

11. GST Compliance Center

Automates monthly GST reconciliation and preparation for filing under Indian tax regulations.

GST Section Data Sources Included Output Action
GSTR-1 Outward Supplies All sales invoices from Doc Gen classified by B2B (Table 4), B2C (Tables 5/7), and SAC 998361. Export GSTR-1 summary for GST Portal upload.
GSTR-3B Tax Summary Total Outward Tax collected minus Eligible Inward ITC paid on purchase bills & expenses. Generates Net Tax Payable settlement report before the 20th of the month.
ITC Reconciliation Purchase bills and verified expense tax invoices. Flags invoices missing supplier GSTIN to avoid ITC loss.
Net Tax Settlement Formula:
$$ ext{Net GST Payable} = ext{Total Output Tax Liability (CGST+SGST+IGST)} - ext{Total Eligible ITC (CGST+SGST+IGST)}$$

13. Financial Statements & Period Closing

1. Trial Balance

Summarizes debit and credit totals across every account head. Verifies that the ledger is balanced prior to balance sheet generation.

2. Balance Sheet

The permanent financial balance sheet on any given date:

Total Assets ≡ Total Liabilities + Owner's Equity

3. Period Locking & Audit Safeguards

To prevent backdated entries or tampering after monthly accounts have been audited, authorized managers can Lock Accounting Periods. Once locked:

  • No staff can add, edit, or delete vouchers dated in that locked month.
  • All financial actions maintain an immutable audit trail recording user email, IP address, and timestamp.

14. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

SOP 1: Daily Morning Routine (Cashier / Junior Accountant)

  • Open Accounts Dashboard and review Due Today receivables and payables.
  • Physically count cash in the petty cash drawer and ensure it matches the ERP Cash in Hand balance.
  • Follow up with clients who have invoices in the 30+ days overdue bracket.

SOP 2: Client Billing & Payment Collection Flow (Billing Clerk)

  • Verify that the approved invoice has synced from Doc Gen to Sales & Receivables.
  • When client sends payment screenshot, verify bank credit on netbanking portal.
  • Click Record Payment, enter exact UTR number, date, and allocate against the invoice.
  • Email or WhatsApp the updated Customer Statement to the client confirming receipt.

SOP 3: Daily Fleet Fuel & Maintenance Logging (Fleet Ops)

  • Collect fuel receipts and workshop slips from drivers at the end of every trip.
  • Navigate to Expenses $ ightarrow$ Click + Record Expense.
  • Tag the vehicle registration number (e.g. KL-09-AB-1234) in the notes field.
  • Save voucher so fleet costs reflect immediately in the vehicle P&L.

SOP 4: Month-End Financial Closing Checklist (Senior Accountant)

  • Ensure all client invoices for the month have been generated in Doc Gen.
  • Perform Bank Reconciliation (BRS) for all company accounts until the difference is ₹0.00.
  • Enter all pending vendor purchase bills and expense slips.
  • Inspect the GSTR-3B Summary to compute net tax liability before the 20th.
  • Review the Profit & Loss Statement with management.
  • Activate Period Lock for the closed month.

15. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: What if a client pays for 3 different invoices in a single bank transfer?
Answer: In Record Payment, enter the total lump-sum UTR amount. The allocation table allows you to split and apply the funds across all 3 invoices until the total receipt is fully matched.

Q2: How do we handle customer advance deposits before a quotation is approved?
Answer: Post a Receipt Voucher (RV) under General Journal: Debit Bank Account, Credit 2130 Customer Advances. Once the invoice is generated, the advance credit can be applied against the invoice balance.

Q3: Can an accountant edit or delete a posted voucher?
Answer: No. In accordance with standard accounting audit standards, posted vouchers are immutable. If a voucher was entered in error, create a Reversal Voucher or an adjusting Journal Voucher (JV) with a clear explanation in the narration.
