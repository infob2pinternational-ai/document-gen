1. Executive Overview & Operating Philosophy

The Telecalling Operations Module in B2P ONE Portal & ERP is designed to capture, organize, and monitor high-volume outbound and inbound customer communication. It connects frontline telecallers directly with business intelligence dashboards, executive dispatch channels (WhatsApp and Email), and automated cloud spreadsheets without manual administrative overhead.

Core Business Objectives

  • Instant Accountability: Every phone interaction is timestamped and recorded under the logged-in staff member's profile.
  • Zero Lost Follow-ups: Prospects who request a call back or require ongoing nurturing are isolated into an actionable Unresolved Calls queue.
  • Strict Temporal Truth: All activity is anchored to the Indian Standard Time (IST — Asia/Kolkata) calendar day. Daily reports never shift or substitute dates automatically.
  • Frictionless High-Speed Entry: Telecallers can log 80+ calls per day without repetitive data entry using automatic duplicate detection and the 1-click Call Again workflow.
  • One-Click Management Visibility: Supervisors can view separate counts for Follow-ups, Call Backs, and Wrong Numbers, click any dashboard tile to see matching calls, and send structured, numbered daily executive summaries via WhatsApp or Email in seconds.

System Architecture Overview

1. Frontend Interface (React + Vite)

Lightweight, responsive, mobile-first interface optimized for rapid telecalling logging. Includes instant validation, non-blocking duplicate alerts, and interactive KPI tiles.

2. Database Layer (Supabase PostgreSQL)

Primary transactional source of truth stored in telecalling_entries with Row Level Security (RLS), phone normalization indexes, and IST date partitioning.

3. Background Sync Queue

Reliable event queue stored in telecalling_google_sync_queue, buffering every insert or update for background sync to Google Sheets without delaying the telecaller.

4. Multi-Channel Dispatch Engine

Sends numbered WhatsApp messages, triggers Google Apps Script HTML emails to configured management addresses, and exports customized CSV files matching filtered views.

2. Strict IST Date Locking Standard & Date Shift Elimination

CRITICAL SYSTEM RULE: The Daily Report will NEVER automatically shift the report date. If a supervisor selects 24 September 2026, the system queries ONLY entry_date = '2026-09-24'. It will NEVER substitute yesterday, previous business days, UTC dates, or the last populated date.

Why Date Shifts Were Completely Eliminated

In earlier versions, when a supervisor opened the Daily Report early in the morning before any staff had logged calls, the system automatically shifted backwards to show "yesterday's data" or "the last active day." While well-intentioned, this caused extreme confusion:

  • Supervisors mistook yesterday's numbers for today's morning activity.
  • Staff thought previously logged calls were missing or wiped out.
  • Changing a filter unexpectedly bounced the date picker back to previous dates.

Under the current version, the date selector is completely locked to the user's explicit choice. If zero records exist for the selected date, the system displays an honest, transparent confirmation:

No telecalling entries found for 24 September 2026. No calls were logged on 2026-09-24. The report date remains fixed on your selection.

Removal of Manual Date Field from Daily Entry

To prevent human error, backdating, or date discrepancies between client devices and servers, the visible Date field has been completely removed from the Daily Entry form.

Factor Legacy Approach (Deprecated) Current Standard (Enforced)
Date Selection Manual calendar picker on entry form. Completely removed from staff entry screen.
Timezone Calculation Browser UTC / Local PC time. Asia/Kolkata (IST — UTC+05:30) calculated at exact save time.
Midnight Rollover Calls logged after 12:00 AM IST used client timezone. Automatically increments to the new calendar day at 12:00:00 AM IST.

3. Daily Telecalling Entry Workflow & Mandatory Remarks

The Daily Entry screen (TelecallingDailyEntry.tsx) is the core operational cockpit for frontline staff. It is streamlined for maximum typing speed while enforcing strict data hygiene.

Field-by-Field Entry Standard

Field Name Type Requirement Validation & Operating Rules

Contact Person Text Optional Key decision maker, manager, or owner spoken with during the call.
Phone Number Tel Mandatory 10-digit primary mobile number. Auto-normalized and checked for duplicates.
Other Phone Tel Optional Landline, extension, or alternate mobile number.

Why Feedback / Remarks is Strictly Mandatory

In high-volume operations, telecallers frequently select a status (such as Follow-up Required or Interested) but leave the remarks field completely blank. This leads to critical business failures:

  • Another staff member picking up the lead has no idea what was discussed.
  • Supervisors cannot evaluate call quality or customer objections.
  • Prospects become frustrated when asked the same introductory questions twice.
System Validation Rule: If a telecaller attempts to save a record with an empty or whitespace-only feedback field, the form blocks submission with an immediate error:
"Please enter feedback or remarks for this call."
Examples of High-Quality vs. Rejected Remarks

Rejected (Blank / Useless): "Ok", ".", "Call done", ""
Accepted (High Quality): "Spoke with Mr. Suresh (Director). Interested in ISO certification package. Requested proposal by email. Call back on Monday 11:00 AM."
Accepted (Wrong Number): "Person answered stated this is a residential number and has no connection with Apex Trading. Marked wrong number."

4. Duplicate Warning, Call History Search & "Call Again"

A major challenge in telecalling is multiple callers contacting the same prospect on the same day, or failing to recall what was discussed last week. B2P ONE solves this with Feature 8 (Search History) and Feature 6 (Duplicate Warning).

Real-Time Duplicate / Previous Call Warning

As soon as a telecaller types 10 digits of a Phone number or enters a Company Name, the system instantly executes an asynchronous query against the complete telecalling_entries database:

NON-BLOCKING WARNING: If matching records are found, an amber alert banner appears immediately above the form:
⚠️ Previous Call Records Found: Shows the date of the previous call, the telecaller who made it, the status, and the previous feedback remarks.

Crucial Architectural Decision: This alert is strictly non-blocking. It informs the staff member of previous activity but does not prevent them from submitting a new call. Multiple calls to the same prospect are normal in sales follow-ups.

The 1-Click "Call Again" Workflow

Inside both the Previous Call Search results and the Unresolved Calls queue, each record features a blue "Call Again" button.

Data Field Behavior on "Call Again" Click Operational Reason
Company Name Copied Exactly Eliminates retyping company name and prevents spelling variations.
Contact Person Copied Exactly Preserves key decision-maker name.
Phone & Other Phone Copied Exactly Preserves verified telephone numbers.
Location & Email Copied Exactly Preserves demographic and contact metadata.
Call Result / Status Reset to Blank Caller must record the outcome of the new call, not the old one.
Feedback / Remarks Reset to Blank Caller must write fresh notes reflecting the current conversation.
Entry Date Fresh IST Timestamp Automatically assigned the current Asia/Kolkata date upon save.

Triple-Layer Anti-Duplicate & Double-Submission Protection

To guarantee that accidental double-clicks or repeated submissions never create redundant rows in the database or Google Sheets, the system enforces three independent defensive layers:

1. Synchronous Click Lock

The moment a caller clicks Save Entry or Save & Log Another, an in-memory synchronous ref (isSubmittingRef) blocks all subsequent clicks before React can even re-render. All submit buttons are immediately disabled, the label changes to "Saving Call Record...", and a loading spinner appears.

2. Pre-Insert Duplicate Check Modal

Before creating a new database record, the system checks whether an entry with the same Phone number or Company Name was already submitted today (or within the last 10 minutes). If found, an interactive modal prompts the caller with side-by-side comparison:

  • Update Existing Record (Recommended): Overwrites the existing record's status and remarks without appending a duplicate row to Google Sheets.
  • Save as Separate Call: Explicitly logs a distinct secondary interaction.
  • Cancel: Returns to editing.

3. Form Reset & Cache Wipe

After every successful save or update, resetForm() executes immediately. All inputs (Company, Contact, Phone, Status, Feedback) are cleared, preventing the browser from retaining old payloads. Refreshing the browser starts with a completely clean form.

4. Backend Idempotency Cooldown

Even in the event of browser network retries or parallel requests, telecallingService maintains a 15-second de-duplication cache on company_id + entry_date + phone + company_name. Redundant payloads within 15 seconds return the existing entry rather than inserting a duplicate.

6. Quick Filter Chips & Instant Click-to-Filter Architecture

Supervisors and telecallers need to switch between call categories instantly without scrolling through long tables or dropdown menus.

Interactive Tile Click Functionality

Every single dashboard card is interactive:

  1. Click any Tile: Clicking on Appointments (Tile 3) immediately filters the table below to show ONLY the confirmed appointment records.
  2. Active Visual Indicators: The active tile is highlighted with an emphasized colored border, background tint, and an ACTIVE status badge.
  3. Toggle Off: Clicking the active tile a second time clears the filter and restores all records.
  4. Clear Filter Banner: A prominent top banner displays: "Filtered by Status: [Selected Status] (X of Y entries)" with a 1-click Clear Filter button.

Global Metric Stability Architecture

A frequent defect in web reporting occurs when applying a filter re-computes the dashboard KPIs based only on the filtered rows—causing all other KPI numbers to suddenly drop to zero.

How B2P ONE Solved This: The data loader (loadReport) fetches the complete daily dataset from Supabase once. The KPI tiles calculate totals across the entire day's data. When you click Wrong Number, only the displayed rows are filtered in memory. The dashboard cards retain their full numbers (Appointments: 5, Interested: 8, Follow-up: 12), ensuring supervisors never lose overall operational perspective.

7. Unresolved Calls Action Queue & High-Priority Workflows

The Unresolved Calls Queue (Feature 9 & 10) aggregates every interaction from the selected date that still demands operational follow-through.

Centralized Unresolved Status Definition

In src/types/index.ts, unresolved status eligibility is centralized in one strict constant:

export const UNRESOLVED_STATUSES: TelecallingStatus[] = [ 'Follow-up Required', 'Call Back', 'No Answer / No Response', 'Not Reachable / Switched Off', 'Interested / Details Shared' ];

The following statuses are strictly excluded as they represent terminal or closed outcomes:

  • Appointment Confirmed (Converted into field appointment).
  • No Interest (Prospect explicitly opted out).
  • Wrong / Invalid Number (Dead contact; flagged for database cleanup).

Dedicated Action Buttons for Every Unresolved Lead

Each card in the Unresolved Calls view provides 4 rapid-fire actions:

Action Button Icon Function & Behavior
Call 📞 Phone Triggers native device dialer (tel:+91XXXXXXXXXX) on mobile or VOIP software on PC.
WhatsApp 💬 Message Opens WhatsApp Web or mobile app directly to the client's normalized phone number.
Copy Phone 📋 Copy Copies 10-digit number to clipboard with instant visual confirmation ("Copied!").
Call Again 🔄 Refresh Opens Daily Entry pre-filled with company, contact, and phone for a fresh IST log.

9. Contextual CSV Export & Google Sheets Cloud Sync

Data portability and external auditing are supported through Feature 11 (Contextual CSV Export) and continuous Google Sheets Cloud Sync.

Feature 11: Contextual / Filter-Aware CSV Export

A frequent complaint with standard ERP systems is that clicking "Export" downloads the entire multi-year database rather than what the user is currently examining.

Contextual Export Rule: The CSV Export button generates a file containing ONLY the currently filtered records. If a supervisor has filtered by Call Back and telecaller "Anju", the CSV will contain only those specific calls.

Automatic Filename Naming Convention

  • Unfiltered Daily Export: Telecalling_Report_2026-09-24.csv
  • Filtered View Export: Telecalling_Report_2026-09-24_Filtered.csv

Data Hygiene & RFC-4180 Escaping

The CSV generator strictly implements:

  • UTF-8 Byte Order Mark (BOM): Ensures Microsoft Excel opens Indian names and symbols without character corruption.
  • Field Quoting & Escape: Quotes all fields containing commas, line breaks, or double quotes (escaped as "").
  • Excluded Technical IDs: Internal database UUIDs and raw sync payloads are omitted for executive clarity.

Google Sheets Background Cloud Sync Architecture

Every call logged in B2P ONE is automatically queued for cloud synchronization:

  1. Upon save, the record is inserted into telecalling_entries and simultaneously written to telecalling_google_sync_queue with status pending.
  2. A background synchronization service dispatches the queue payload to the Google Apps Script Web App.
  3. The Google Apps Script appends the row to the organization's master Google Sheet.
  4. If the telecaller's internet connection drops, the queue holds the record offline and retries automatically once connectivity is restored—guaranteeing zero lost records.

11. Staff Daily Checklist & Operational Do's and Don'ts

DO'S (Mandatory Practices)

  • Always search phone first: Check if a colleague called the prospect earlier today to avoid duplicate calling.
  • Write meaningful feedback: Always note the contact's job title, objections, specific requirements, and promised next steps.
  • Use "Call Again" for callbacks: Save time and preserve data integrity by using the 1-click button instead of typing from scratch.
  • Clear unresolved items daily: Review your Unresolved queue before leaving for the day.
  • Mark Wrong Numbers accurately: Flag invalid or disconnected numbers so the database remains clean.

DON'TS (Strictly Prohibited)

  • Do NOT enter placeholder remarks: Entering dots, "ok", or single words is a violation of operational standards.
  • Do NOT mark Appointments without agreement: Only use Appointment Confirmed when the client agreed to an exact date/time.
  • Do NOT ignore duplicate warnings: When an amber alert appears, read the previous feedback before proceeding.
  • Do NOT use browser UTC extensions: Do not tamper with PC clock or timezone settings; the server enforces IST.
  • Do NOT share reports outside authorized channels: Telecalling data contains confidential customer contact details.
